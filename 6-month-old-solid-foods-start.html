Starting Solid Foods at 6 Months: A Guide for Parents

The introduction of solid foods is a major milestone, and the recommended time to start is around 6 months of age. This guide will help you recognize the signs of readiness and how to begin safely.

When to Start and Signs of Readiness

Infants are usually ready to eat solid foods at about 6 months of age . However, it&#x27;s important to check with your health care provider for the best time for your baby to start . Before 6 months, a baby&#x27;s digestive system may not be mature enough, and starting solids too early can increase the risk of choking and other problems.

Signs your baby is ready for solids include:

Sitting up with little or no support

Good head and neck control

Reaching for and putting objects in their mouth

Showing interest in food (opening their mouth, watching you eat)
