2-Year-Old Choking Hazards: Foods to Avoid

At 2 years old, children are still at high risk for choking because they are learning to chew and swallow and have small airways. The National Institutes of Health (NIH) specifically lists hot dogs, nuts, and grapes as common choking hazards for young children .
