Iron-Rich Foods for Your 18-Month-Old: A Parent's Guide

Iron is a vital mineral that supports your child's healthy growth and development, especially for brain development and the production of healthy red blood cells . An 18-month-old needs about 7 to 9 mg of iron per day . Ensuring your child gets enough iron is crucial, as iron deficiency is one of the most common nutritional deficiencies in toddlers and can lead to problems with learning and attention if left untreated .
