Probiotics for Your 6-Month-Old: What the Science Says

Probiotics are often marketed as a way to support digestive health, but when it comes to infants, the evidence is limited and the recommendations are cautious.

What the Evidence Shows

The sources provided do not contain robust evidence to support the use of probiotics in 6-month-old infants for general health or specific conditions.

No evidence for colic: The only specific mention of probiotics in the provided sources states that probiotic supplements are not recommended for colic and that there is no evidence they help with this condition in infants (Source: NHS).

Bowel Movements and Digestive Health

While probiotics are sometimes used to address digestive issues like diarrhea or constipation, the sources note that these conditions can have various causes. If your 6-month-old is experiencing persistent digestive issues, it is important to seek professional medical advice to determine the underlying cause rather than relying on supplements.

When to See a Doctor

If your 6-month-old is experiencing digestive issues — whether constipation, diarrhea, or general feeding problems — it's important to seek professional medical advice rather than trying over-the-counter supplements. A pediatrician can:

Evaluate your baby's symptoms and identify underlying causes.

Recommend evidence-based dietary changes.

Advise on any appropriate treatments, which may include probiotics in specific cases, but only if supported by evidence and tailored to your baby's needs.

If you are worried about your baby, call your health care provider right away.
