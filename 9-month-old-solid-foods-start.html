Diet for a 9-Month-Old with Reflux

At 9 months old, your baby is eating a variety of solids, and dietary changes can help manage reflux. Gastroesophageal reflux (GER) occurs when food or stomach acid flows back from your child&#x27;s stomach into the esophagus. Occasional reflux is common and usually not a problem. However, if it causes pain, feeding problems, or irritation of the esophagus, it may be Gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD) (Source: NIH).

Key Dietary Strategies

Smaller, More Frequent Meals

Overfeeding is a key risk factor for reflux. Give smaller, more frequent feedings to prevent the stomach from overfilling, which can push food back up.

Texture and Consistency

For a 9-month-old who is eating solids, consider the texture of foods. While the sources do not specify a particular consistency for this age, thicker foods can sometimes help reduce reflux.

Elimination Diets

In some cases, food allergies or sensitivities can cause or worsen reflux. Dietary changes for reflux include:

Elimination Diet

If your child is on an elimination diet, they stop eating and drinking certain foods and beverages for several weeks. If they are feeling better, you add the foods back to their diet one at a time (Source: NIH).

Elemental Diet

With this diet, you stop eating and drinking all proteins (Source: NIH). This is typically reserved for more severe cases and should only be done under strict medical supervision.

When to See a Doctor

If your 9-month-old has reflux that does not improve, or if they have any of the following signs, it&#x27;s important to consult a pediatrician:

Poor weight gain and growth (Source: NIH)

Reflux that does not get better with medicines (Source: NIH)

Trouble swallowing, especially with solid foods (Source: NIH)

Food getting stuck in the esophagus (Source: NIH)

Your child&#x27;s health care provider may diagnose GERD if the reflux causes pain, feeding problems, or irritation of the esophagus (Source: NIH).

If you are worried about your baby, call your health care provider right away.
