Healthy Snacks for Your 4-Year-Old

Food provides the energy and nutrients that young children need to be healthy (Source: NIH). At 4 years old, your child is growing rapidly and needs a steady supply of energy and nutrients throughout the day. Offering healthy snacks is essential to fill the gap between meals and ensure they are getting the nutrition they need.

Why Snacks Are Important

Snacks are more than just a way to keep your child from getting hungry and cranky. They are an opportunity to pack in extra nutrients. Offering a balanced snack two to three times a day helps maintain stable blood sugar levels, supports their physical growth, and fuels their rapidly developing brain. A child's serving size is generally about one-quarter to one-third of an adult portion.

Healthy Snack Ideas

Here are some practical and nutritious snack ideas for your 4-year-old.

Fresh fruit: Offer soft, bite-sized pieces of fruit like bananas, ripe peaches, pears, or cooked apple.

Vegetables: Steamed and diced carrots, sweet potatoes, or green beans. Offer raw veggies with dip for older children.

Yogurt: Plain, unsweetened whole milk yogurt is a fantastic snack packed with calcium and protein.

Crackers: Offer whole-grain crackers or rice cakes.

Cheese: Small cubes or slices of mild cheese.

Eggs: Hard-boiled or scrambled eggs are a great source of protein.

A Note on Dental Health

Healthy teeth are important to your child's overall health (Source: NIH). It's essential to keep oral health in mind when choosing snacks. A key guideline is to provide healthy foods and limit sweet snacks and drinks (Source: NIH). Frequent snacking on sugary foods or drinks can significantly increase the risk of cavities.

To protect your child's teeth:

Limit sweet snacks and drinks.

Offer drinks with meals and snacks.

Encourage drinking water and limit sugary drinks like juice and soda.

Safety Tips

Supervise your child while they are eating.

Prepare food safely: Always cut food into manageable pieces to avoid choking hazards.
