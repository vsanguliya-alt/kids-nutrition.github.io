How Much Milk Does an 18-Month-Old Need? A Guide for Parents

At 18 months, milk is still an important part of your toddler&#x27;s diet, but it&#x27;s now a drink, not a meal. Finding the right balance is key to ensuring your child gets the nutrition they need without filling up on milk.

The Recommended Daily Amount
