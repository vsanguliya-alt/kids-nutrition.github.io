How Much Milk Does an 18-Month-Old Need? A Guide for Parents

At 18 months, milk is still an important part of your toddler's diet, but it's now a drink, not a meal. Finding the right balance is key to ensuring your child gets the nutrition they need without filling up on milk.

The Recommended Daily Amount

For an 18-month-old, the recommended daily intake of cow's milk is generally between 400ml and 480ml (approximately 1 ⅔ to 2 cups) . This amount is often echoed by other health organizations, with some recommending a range of 360ml to 480ml (1.5 to 2 cups) .
