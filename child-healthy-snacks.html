Healthy Snacks for Your Child: A Guide for Parents

Food provides the energy and nutrients that young children need to be healthy (Source: NIH). Offering healthy snacks is an excellent way to ensure your child gets the nutrition they need to grow, learn, and stay healthy. A healthy diet helps children grow and learn (Source: NIH).

Healthy Snack Ideas

Here are some healthy, child-friendly snack ideas:

Fruits: Offer fresh, canned, or dried fruit. Good choices include apples, bananas, berries, oranges, and pears.

Vegetables: Offer raw or cooked vegetables. Good choices include carrots, celery, cucumbers, bell peppers, and cherry tomatoes.

Yogurt: Plain, unsweetened yogurt is a great source of calcium and protein.

Cheese: Offer cheese cubes or string cheese.

Crackers: Offer whole-grain crackers or rice cakes.

Nuts and seeds: Offer unsalted nuts and seeds, if age-appropriate.

Eggs: Hard-boiled or scrambled eggs are a great source of protein.

Tips for Healthy Snacking

Make half of what is on your child&#x27;s plate fruits and vegetables (Source: NIH).

Choose healthy sources of protein, such as lean meat, nuts, and eggs (Source: NIH).
