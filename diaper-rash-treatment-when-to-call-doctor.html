Diaper Rash: When to Call the Doctor

Understanding Diaper Rash

Diaper rash is a common inflammatory skin condition in the diaper area of infants, caused by prolonged exposure to urine and feces, moisture, friction, and elevated skin pH . While most cases resolve with home care, certain signs indicate it's time to seek medical advice .

When to Call the Doctor

Call your pediatrician if your baby's diaper rash:

Does not improve after 2 to 3 days of home treatment

Gets worse despite home care

Spreads beyond the diaper area to the abdomen, back, arms, or face

Is severe or unusual in appearance

Occurs in the first 6 weeks after birth

Is mainly in the skin folds — this may indicate a yeast (Candida) infection requiring different treatment

Has pimples, blisters, ulcers, or pus-filled sores

Bleeds, oozes fluid, or has crusting

Causes pain or burning when your baby passes urine or stool

Your baby also has a fever

Signs of Infection

Seek immediate medical care if you notice signs of a bacterial infection :

Red streaks leading from the rash

Increased swelling, warmth, or redness

Pus draining from the rash area

Significant scratching or distress

Trust Your Instincts

If you are worried about your baby's rash, do not hesitate to call your health care provider right away .
