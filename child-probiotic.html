Probiotics for Your Child: What the Science Says

Probiotics are often marketed as a way to support digestive health, but when it comes to children, the evidence is limited and the recommendations are cautious.

What the Evidence Shows

The sources provided do not contain robust evidence to support the use of probiotics in children for general health or specific conditions.

No evidence for colic: The only specific mention of probiotics in the provided sources states that probiotic supplements are not recommended for colic and that there is no evidence they help with this condition in infants (Source: NHS).

Digestive Health and When to See a Doctor

While probiotics are sometimes used to address digestive issues like diarrhea or constipation, the sources note that these conditions can have various causes. It&#x27;s important to contact a provider right away if your child has any of the following symptoms:

Change in mental state, such as irritability or lack of energy

Diarrhea lasting more than 2 days

Six or more loose stools in a day

Severe pain in the abdomen (belly) or rectum

Stools that are black and tarry or contain blood or pus

Symptoms of dehydration, such as thirst, dry mouth, headache, dark-colored urine, and urinating less than normal (Source: NIH)

If your child is experiencing persistent digestive issues, it is important to seek professional medical advice to determine the underlying cause rather than relying on supplements.

When to See a Doctor

If your child is experiencing digestive issues — whether constipation, diarrhea, or general feeding problems — it&#x27;s important to seek professional medical advice rather than trying over-the-counter supplements. A pediatrician can:

Evaluate your child&#x27;s symptoms and identify underlying causes.

Recommend evidence-based dietary changes.

Advise on any appropriate treatments, which may include probiotics in specific cases, but only if supported by evidence and tailored to your child&#x27;s needs.

If you are worried about your child, call your health care provider right away.
