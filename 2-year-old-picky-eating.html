2-Year-Old Picky Eating: A Parent's Guide

Picky eating is a common and normal phase for many 2-year-olds. At this age, children are developing their own preferences and asserting their independence, which can lead to unpredictable and selective appetites. While it can be frustrating, understanding why it happens and how to respond can make mealtimes more pleasant for everyone.
