Probiotics for a 2-Year-Old: A Guide for Parents

Probiotics are live bacteria and yeasts that are good for digestive health, often found in supplements and some foods like yogurt. They can be helpful for treating diarrhea in children, particularly diarrhea related to antibiotics or viral infections. However, there is no evidence they help with colic .

🔬 Evidence on Probiotics

VERIFIED: Probiotics are not recommended for treating colic in infants due to a lack of evidence, according to the NHS .

💡 What to Do

RECOMMENDATION: Always consult your pediatrician before giving any supplement, including probiotics, to your child. They can provide personalized advice based on your child's specific health needs, especially if your child has a weakened immune system or is critically ill .
